About this data
  • Phone version of the Sana Trybe dashboard, with the same data: Trybe's Sold items and Sessions exports pulled 23 Nov 2026. Trybe starts 18 Mar 2026; November runs to the 22nd. Weeks run Monday to Sunday from 28 Sep; the current week runs to yesterday.
  • Net sales are after discounts, before tax. Passes paid with a gift card or multi-pass show $0 on the visit, since Trybe counts that money when the card or bundle sells.
  • Occupancy is guests booked against slot capacity: 5 per 2.5-hour SANA Pass slot, a slot every 15 minutes. Test sessions and old Book4Time passes are left out.
  • Booking lead is order time to session start. Front-desk passes rung in on arrival count as walk-ins. Guest and repeat counts use the booking email; no names or emails are stored.
Data: Trybe Sold items and Sessions exports, pulled 23 November 2026. Open the desktop version
