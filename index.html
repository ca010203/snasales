About this data
  • A fixed copy of Trybe's Sold items and Sessions exports for the Sana site, pulled on 18 November 2026. Trybe's history starts on 18 March 2026, so March is a part month; November runs to the 17th. Weeks run Monday to Sunday from the week of 28 September, and the current week runs to yesterday. Earlier bookings were in Book4Time and are not here.
  • Net sales are Trybe's net total: after discounts, before tax. Day passes paid with a gift card or a multi-pass bundle show no net sales on the visit, because Trybe counts that money when the card or bundle is sold, so net per guest understates what each visit is worth.
  • Guests are SANA Pass session guests by visit date. Occupancy is guests booked against slot capacity from the Sessions export (5 guests per 2.5-hour slot, a slot every 15 minutes); test sessions and the old Book4Time day passes are left out.
  • Booking lead is the time from the order being placed in Trybe to the start of the session. Passes rung in at the front desk as guests arrive count as walk-ins. Online covers trybe.com bookings; Reserve with Google is shown on its own.
  • Guest counts and repeat visits use the email on the booking, so walk-ins without an email are left out of the Guests tab. No names or emails are stored in this dashboard.
